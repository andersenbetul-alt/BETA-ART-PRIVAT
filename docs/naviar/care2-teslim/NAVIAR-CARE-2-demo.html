Studio decision record & verification · English

Example brief

5 September 2026. Build a portable, multilingual everyday-support prototype for older adults and relatives. Demonstrate service discovery, scope and price information, an introductory-call request, confirmation, edits and cancellation. Assumptions: developer available; early concept; no verified operating area, prices, staff, merchant account or budget. These are demonstration assumptions, not confirmed business facts.

Platform decision

Selected: custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript for this offline demonstration. It preserves all three languages and behavior in one editable file with no dependencies. Tradeoff: developer-led maintenance and separately engineered integrations. Vercel is a possible host, not the development framework. No existing repository was supplied or changed. Before any migration, inspect the actual repository, content, URLs and integrations; compare incremental work against rebuild cost, redirects and maintenance.

Journey and service operations

Discovery → scope and price expectations → request → local confirmation → edit/cancel. A real service coordinator must assess geography, capacity and suitability before offering a confirmed appointment. The customer receiving help must remain involved when a relative initiates contact. Proposed scope excludes clinical care.

Design and content

Warm paper background, forest-green text, serif headings, system body font; 18px body text; 48px controls; 20–64px spacing; visible focus; explicit labels and errors. Responsive one-column layout below 750px. Original decorative home illustration, no stock customer claims. Norwegian Bokmål, English and Turkish copy includes navigation, services, validation and confirmation. Language state uses ?lang=nb|en|tr. Native-language editorial review remains pending.

Integration and launch handoff

Booking: this is a request simulation, with no dates or availability promises. Before launch, implement Europe/Oslo timezone handling, available slots, concurrency protection, cancellation rules and tested confirmation delivery. Email: draft only—“We received your request for [service]. This is not yet a confirmed appointment. We will contact you at [contact].” Delivery, sender authentication, bounce handling and verified receipt are not configured. Payments: no checkout or price invented; establish service price, taxes, merchant eligibility, refunds and sandbox success/failure flows before activation.

Privacy: the demo sends and persists no form data, and runs no analytics. Before real collection, establish the controller, purpose, lawful basis, retention, processors, access controls and appropriate privacy notices with current official guidance. No GDPR or security certification is claimed. Avoid health-information free text in initial contact. Analytics proposal: measure service-detail visits and completed requests in aggregate only after deciding the lawful configuration. Observed completion rates are not predictions of individual need. No prediction model is implemented.

SEO: deliberately noindex for the demo. Production needs verified domain, separately renderable locale pages, canonical/hreflang mappings, sitemap and factual business metadata. No fabricated contact details, reviews, availability or credentials.

Verification record

Verified 2026-09-05: JavaScript syntax, unique IDs, internal anchor targets, complete translation bindings in all three languages, and 21 assertions for locale rendering and request/error/edit/cancel logic using a simulated DOM. No network or browser storage APIs present. Calculated contrast ratios for main text, secondary text, buttons and error text: 11.37, 6.07, 12.08, 8.13:1. These source and logic checks do not establish rendered accessibility. Browser connection failed twice; mobile/desktop rendering, actual keyboard navigation, zoom, overflow and assistive technology checks remain unverified. No transactions or external messages were executed. This is a prototype, not a live service.

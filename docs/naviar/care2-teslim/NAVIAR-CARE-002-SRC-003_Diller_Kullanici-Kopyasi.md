# Care in the language you think in

Describing pain is hard enough in your first language. We make sure the language barrier is ours to solve, not yours.

**113**

languages we can consult in

**44**

spoken natively by doctors on the platform

**24/7**

medical interpreters on call for the rest

**How it works**

## Two ways we get you a consultation in your language

**Doctor speaks it**

### Straight to a doctor who shares your language

For these languages we have clinicians who consult natively. Nothing is relayed through anyone else — you speak, the doctor answers, in the same language.

**Interpreter joins**

### A medical interpreter on the same call

For every other language, a trained medical interpreter joins within minutes. They interpret clinical terms accurately — this is not machine translation, and it is never a family member left to explain a diagnosis.

svg

**Your written notes come in your language too**

The summary, the advice and any referral are written in the language you were seen in, so you still have it after the call ends.

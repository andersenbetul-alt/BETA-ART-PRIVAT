How pre-consultation advisory works

You describe the problem, the advisor tells you where to go. That's it.

Step 1
Pick a specialty area
Not sure? Use the symptom checker first — it will suggest a specialty for you.
Step 2
Choose your language
Select your preferred language. Advisors who speak it appear at the top of the list.
Step 3
Book or write
Schedule a short video call or send a written message — whichever you prefer. No waiting room.
Step 4
Get your routing letter
The advisor tells you which specialist, clinic, or department to go to, and why. You arrive prepared.

Reach a specialist advisor in your own language and arrive at the right department the first time.

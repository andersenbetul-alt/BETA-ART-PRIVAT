Scope of service

⚠️
NaviarCare advisors provide navigational guidance only. They help you decide which type of specialist to consult next — they do not diagnose, prescribe, or issue referrals. Every session is labelled a pre-consultation advisory, and every advisor agrees to this scope before their profile is activated.

NaviarCare is a marketplace that connects people with licensed clinicians acting in a pre-consultation advisory capacity. An advisory session helps a user answer the question "which type of specialist should I see?" based on the symptoms they describe. The answer is a directional recommendation — a direction, not a clinical finding.

What advisors do

  • Listen to described symptoms and medical history
  • Suggest which medical specialty is likely most relevant
  • Explain what different specialists do and when they are appropriate
  • Indicate the level of urgency (routine, prompt, urgent, emergency)
  • Describe how to prepare for a specialist visit

What advisors do not do

  • Diagnose a condition or rule one out
  • Prescribe or recommend specific medications
  • Issue referral letters or sick-leave certificates
  • Make clinical decisions that determine treatment
  • Act as a patient's primary care clinician

This scope is not a limitation — it is the design. A navigator who tells you to see a cardiologist is providing value precisely because they do not also become your cardiologist.

EU Medical Device Regulation (MDR) position

EU 2017/745 MDR MDCG 2019-11 MDCG 2024-6

The EU Medical Device Regulation classifies certain health software as Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) when it "influences clinical management" through a clinical decision-support algorithm. The European Commission's guidance document MDCG 2019-11 sets out the test: software that routes patients based on geography, availability, or expressed preference is outside SaMD scope. Software that assesses symptoms clinically and routes based on that assessment is inside SaMD scope and requires CE certification at minimum Class I (MDR Annex II/III).

How NaviarCare is positioned

The symptom checker on NaviarCare is a user-directed navigation aid. It prompts the user to describe their symptoms and suggests a specialty area — but the routing decision is made by the human advisor in a pre-consultation session, not by an autonomous algorithm. The advisor's output is advisory, not a clinical decision.

This positions the platform outside the SaMD scope as described in MDCG 2019-11, because:

  • No autonomous algorithm makes a clinical determination
  • The human advisor is licensed and bears individual professional liability
  • The platform does not substitute for a clinical consultation — it precedes one
  • The routing output is a suggestion confirmed by user choice, not a treatment pathway

We maintain this position actively. Any feature that would move symptom assessment into algorithm-driven clinical routing is reviewed by legal counsel before release. We do not add features to the symptom checker without that review.

What this means in practice

NaviarCare does not carry a CE medical device mark, and is not registered as a medical device with any competent authority. If a future version of the platform includes an autonomous clinical routing algorithm, it would require a conformity assessment before release in the EU/EEA.

Country-by-country regulatory position

Health services regulation varies significantly by country. Below is our current operational status and the key legal basis in the markets we are building towards.

🇳🇴 Norway — Active pilot market

Helsepersonelloven (1999) Pasientjournalloven Normen 7.x E-consultation rules (2023)

Norway is our primary pilot market. Key rules that govern the service:

  • Autorisasjon requirement: Clinicians providing pre-consultation advisory sessions to Norwegian patients must hold Norwegian authorisation (autorisasjon) from Helsedirektoratet. We verify this during onboarding — advisors without Norwegian autorisasjon are not shown to users in Norway.
  • E-consultation permanently legalised (2023): The temporary COVID-era rules permitting e-consultation and remote sick-leave were made permanent in 2023. NaviarCare's advisory model is fully within the legal framework.
  • Normen: Norway's information security standard for health (Normen 7.x) applies to health IT systems connected to Norwegian health infrastructure. We follow Normen access-control and logging requirements. Direct integration with the national health record (Pasientjournal) is not currently planned — if added, a full Normen compliance audit is required.

🇹🇷 Turkey — Requires institutional partnership

Yönetmelik 2022 (Uzaktan Sağlık Hizmetleri) Ministry of Health approval

Turkey's 2022 Remote Healthcare Services Regulation (Uzaktan Sağlık Hizmetleri Yönetmeliği) requires that remote medical consultations be delivered through a licensed Turkish healthcare institution, not through an individual practitioner operating from an unlicensed platform. The regulation further requires that the software platform itself receive Ministry of Health approval before it is used for consultations with Turkish patients.

NaviarCare cannot operate directly in Turkey at this time. Entry into the Turkish market requires:

  • Partnership with a Ministry-licensed healthcare institution that assumes clinical oversight responsibility
  • Ministry of Health software approval for the NaviarCare platform
  • Advisors on Turkey-facing sessions to hold relevant Turkish practitioner registration

Turkey entry is planned for Phase 2, subject to completing the above institutional and regulatory steps. Turkish users who reach the platform in the meantime are shown a notice explaining that the service is not yet available in their country.

🇪🇺 European Union — Framework applies

EU 2017/745 MDR GDPR 2016/679 AI Act 2024

NaviarCare is registered in Norway and operates within the EU/EEA legal framework. The General Data Protection Regulation applies to all users. Health data is special category data under GDPR Article 9 — see the Data section below.

The EU AI Act (applicable from 2026) classifies medical-purpose AI. Because our symptom checker does not make autonomous clinical decisions, it is not expected to fall under high-risk AI (Annex III) — it is informational software where a licensed human professional makes the routing recommendation. We will seek legal opinion before any AI-assisted advisory features are introduced.

🌍 Other markets

We have reviewed global telemedicine regulatory models including the United States (federal HIPAA + state-by-state telemedicine practice standards), the United Kingdom (CQC registration requirement), Canada (provincial licensing), Australia (AHPRA registration), and Gulf Cooperation Council states (Ministry of Health licensing per country).

We do not currently offer the service in these markets. Expansion requires country-specific regulatory review in each case. We will not market to patients in a country where we have not completed that review.

Advisor verification and licensing

Who can become an advisor

Every advisor listed on NaviarCare is a licensed medical professional — a medical doctor (MD or equivalent) with an active registration in at least one recognised national register. We do not list unlicensed practitioners, wellness coaches, or health advisors without a recognised medical qualification.

Verification steps

  • Active registration check against the issuing authority's public register
  • Declared specialty confirmed against the scope of that registration
  • Professional indemnity insurance document covering advisory and telemedicine activity
  • Signed declaration of scope: routing and navigational guidance only, no diagnosis
  • Identity verification (government-issued ID)

For advisors intending to serve Norwegian patients: Norwegian autorisasjon verified via Helsedirektoratets register. Profiles without this authorisation are filtered from search results shown to Norway-based users.

Ongoing obligations

Advisors agree to notify NaviarCare immediately of any change to their licence status, restriction, investigation, or suspension by any regulatory body. NaviarCare conducts periodic re-verification and may suspend a profile at any time pending investigation of a complaint.

Emergency disclaimer

🚨
If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now. NaviarCare is not an emergency service and cannot dispatch emergency responders.

Our symptom intake screen checks for warning signs including severe chest pain, difficulty breathing, signs of stroke, severe allergic reaction, and loss of consciousness. When it identifies one, it instructs the user to stop and contact emergency services. This check does not replace clinical judgement and is not exhaustive.

If an advisor believes a user needs emergency care during a session, they will say so directly and end the advisory session. They are not in a position to call emergency services on your behalf.

Data, privacy and GDPR Article 9

GDPR 2016/679 Art. 9 — special category data Pasientjournalloven (Norway)

Health data is special category data

Information about your health — including symptoms you describe, body systems affected, or the specialties you are directed towards — is special category personal data under GDPR Article 9. This category attracts the highest level of data protection under European law. We process it only on the lawful basis of your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)), given when you proceed from the symptom checker to book a session.

Symptom checker

The symptom checker runs entirely in your browser. Your answers are held in memory only and are not transmitted to our servers while you are completing the check. If you proceed to a session, you are shown a summary of your answers and asked to confirm what to share. Nothing is shared without that step.

Session data

Session records — the advisor's navigational recommendation and relevant notes — are stored encrypted and accessed only by the advisor you selected and, where required by law, a nominated data processor acting under a Data Processing Agreement. They are not accessible by other advisors or by NaviarCare staff without a specific support reason.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell health data or any personal data
  • We do not share health data with insurers, employers, or marketers
  • We do not use consultation data to train AI models without your explicit separate consent
  • We do not build advertising profiles from health information
  • We do not require account creation to use the symptom checker

Retention

Session records are retained for the minimum period required by the applicable health data regulation in the patient's country and in Norway. For Norwegian patients, this is governed by Pasientjournalloven. Records are deleted or irreversibly anonymised at the end of the retention period. You may request deletion earlier — we will comply unless a legal obligation prevents it.

Your rights

Under GDPR you have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data; the right to data portability; and the right to object to processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us via the contact form. We will respond within 30 days.

Liability and platform role

Marketplace intermediary

NaviarCare is a marketplace platform that provides infrastructure for independent licensed clinicians to offer pre-consultation advisory services. It is not a healthcare provider. It does not employ the advisors listed on the platform, and it does not supervise their clinical judgement.

Each advisor is an independent professional who holds their own licence, their own professional indemnity insurance, and their own responsibility for the guidance they give. NaviarCare's liability for advisory content is limited to that of a marketplace intermediary — we are responsible for the accuracy of the information displayed about an advisor, the verification steps we carry out, and the functioning of the platform.

Clinical responsibility

Clinical responsibility for each advisory session rests entirely with the licensed professional who conducts it. If you act on guidance from an advisor and that guidance is wrong, your recourse is to the advisor and their insurer — not to NaviarCare as a platform. We make this clear before every session.

Platform limitations

NaviarCare does not guarantee availability of any particular advisor, accuracy of any routing suggestion, or fitness of any advisory session for any specific medical purpose. Advisory sessions are a supplement to, not a substitute for, professional medical care.

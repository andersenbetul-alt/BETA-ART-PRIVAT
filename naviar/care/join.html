What "routing only" means in practice

A patient describes chest pain. You tell them whether to go to a cardiologist, their GP, or A&E — and how urgently. You do not say what is causing the pain.

This positioning keeps your liability low. NaviarCare is a marketplace intermediary — your professional indemnity is with your insurer, not with the platform.

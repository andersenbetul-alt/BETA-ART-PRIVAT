How it works

Four steps from "something is wrong" to the right doctor. No referral letters, no waiting rooms, and no having to know which specialist to ask for.

Why Naviar Care

Built for whoever is furthest from care. Most people do not delay treatment because they do not care. They delay because they cannot get through, cannot explain, or do not know who to ask.

A doctor in minutes, not weeks
Real clinicians are on call around the clock. Most patients are talking to one inside ten minutes.
Care in your own language
Speak to a doctor who shares your language, or bring in a medical interpreter on the same call.
Sent to the right specialist first time

Emergencies spotted early

You arrive already prepared

The queue problem

Most of that wait was never necessary. People spend a morning in a hospital queue to be told they are in the wrong department. They get sent away to book with someone else, and start again.

Almost none of that is a medical problem. It is a sorting problem, and it can be solved before anyone leaves the house.

Know which department handles your problem before you travel
Arrive with your history, medicines and questions written down
Find out in two minutes whether this needs a hospital at all
Free up the queue for the people who genuinely need to be in it
You do not have to book with us to use this The symptom check and the summary it produces are free, run entirely on your own device, and are yours to keep. Print it and take it to your own doctor if that is what you need. We would rather you arrived at the right place prepared than paid us for a consultation you did not need.

For clinicians

Somebody out there speaks your language and cannot find a doctor.

If you are a licensed doctor, you can register your specialty, your languages and your hours, and help the people our routing sends you. They arrive having already described their symptoms, so you start with the history rather than a blank page.

  • ✓ You set your own fee and keep all of it
  • ✓ Our platform fee is flat — never a share of your professional fee
  • ✓ No minimum hours and no exclusivity
  • ✓ Every licence verified before a profile goes live
Register as a doctor

Nearly every clinician has watched someone struggle through an appointment in a language they barely speak, and known the consultation was worse for it. If you share a language with a patient, you are often the difference between a useful appointment and a wasted one — and there are far more of those patients than there are of you.

Naviar Care does not replace emergency services If someone is unconscious, struggling to breathe, bleeding heavily, or you think they are having a heart attack or stroke, call your local emergency number now. Our intake will tell you the same thing the moment it spots a warning sign.
